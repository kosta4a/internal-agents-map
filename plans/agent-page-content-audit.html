Agent pages: content consistency audit

The pages use a shared template, but their records answer different editorial questions. Missing sections can mean unreported information, incomplete extraction, or content placed in another field.

The largest opportunity is to establish a shared set of questions for every entry, then distinguish those states explicitly. More headings alone would not make the pages comparable.

The meaningful differences underneath the headings

Workflow fields contain several different kinds of content

The schema calls these items primitives, while the page calls them How it works. Across 71 live items, the content ranges from a real task sequence to architecture components, permission constraints, design principles, and near-repetitions of the summary. The renderer groups by field name; it does not identify the editorial function of a sentence.

Bundled records and supervision use different units

The live catalog contains 15 task agents, 6 background agents, 5 agent systems, 10 platforms, 2 orchestration systems, and 2 supporting patterns. It mixes 28 scaled deployments, 10 deployed entries, and 2 research entries. A platform, a research framework, and one narrow production agent cannot be judged against an identical execution narrative.

Nevertheless, every live page has exactly one supervision scope. Several combine workflows: Forge/Mux, Honk/Xirp, Aquifer/River, Sphera/Atlas/Morphex, Brex, and Flux. A coding PR boundary does not answer how their other workflows act or escalate. Nine boundaries remain unknown; that is explicit uncertainty, not a missing section. Some classifications are already being reconciled locally under Plan 008.

“Human involvement” in the header and a scoped attention boundary in the body are different classifications. Readers need the concrete action: who requests, approves, reviews, or handles an exception, and whether a tool action has already taken effect. A numeric level alone does not answer that.

Evidence traceability is stronger than evidence comparability

All 98 live source records have preserved captures. They comprise 70 first-party, 7 direct-participant, 7 secondary, and 14 community records. Across 578 claims there are 681 evidence links; 464 lack a locator. A page-level citation may be acceptable, but a missing locator makes exact support harder to inspect.

The 144 catalog-judgment claims include operating-model judgments and interpretations. Ordinary claim rendering does not display supporting citations or provenance inline; these remain in the disclosure. Lessons hide field/kind labels, so the collective heading carries much of the distinction. This makes interpretation-heavy and evidence-heavy pages appear more alike while scanning. src/components/Claim.astro:35; src/components/ResearchDetails.astro:13. A content checklist that works across the catalog

Require an answer or a documented evidence state for each question, rather than a fixed amount of prose. Keep the common reading order while allowing platform and supporting-system pages to answer the appropriate version of the question.

Reader’s questionMinimum useful contentHow broad/supporting systems differ
What is this, and why does the organization use it?Internal user, recurring problem, task/output, and the organization’s own adaptation. Separate a general product capability from documented internal use.Name the platform/component’s responsibility and which internal systems consume it.
What happens during a run?Trigger/input → relevant context → model/tool actions → validation → output. Preserve branches/retries where material. Omit unsupported steps.Use a named representative workflow or a request/access contract. Do not invent one universal platform run.
What do people do, and when?Requester, approval point, review/acceptance, exception handling, and what changes before approval. Explain the evidence behind the scoped classification.Describe distinct controls by workflow; distinguish platform access/configuration from downstream task review.
What is the implementation?Model, harness, execution/isolation, tools, context sources, state, identity/credentials, and interfaces. Each field should answer its own question.Mark genuinely inapplicable components with a reason; identify inherited or delegated responsibilities.
How is successful or failed work recognized?Tests/evaluations, deterministic gates, retries, refusal/escalation, recovery/rollback if reported. Distinguish configured safeguards from measured reliability.Specify the guarantees and failure behavior of the shared component and its caller.
What has been observed?Subject/component, observation type, date/window, value or statement, baseline, denominator, method, and limitations where supplied. One observation should not be counted twice.Keep platform adoption and component task outcomes separate; do not aggregate overlapping counts.
What can we learn, and how well is it supported?Separate company practice/opinion, catalog interpretation, and known limitation. Cite the supporting passage beside the claim or through an obvious nearby route.A useful method can be documented without measured business impact; say exactly which evidence exists.
Use four evidence states. Reported; reviewed but unreported in the checked sources; not applicable with a reason; not yet reviewed/extracted. Keep conflicting reports as explicit claim relations. The last state is primarily an editorial backlog signal; it should not be translated to N/A or to “the system does not do this.”

For the Results area, separate observations from lessons and identify whether an observation is effectiveness, adoption/output, cost/latency, implementation scale, or an estimate/target. For pages without measured outcomes, a concise “No measured outcomes documented in the reviewed sources” is useful only after that source review has actually been performed.

The existing template and intake instructions already establish provenance and unknown handling. Their missing layer is coverage: they do not require an editorial disposition for every page question, and structural tests cannot tell whether a preserved source’s implementation section was fully used. templates/agent.yaml:30; .claude/skills/add-agent-from-url/SKILL.md:25; tests/web/entry-view.test.ts:22.
